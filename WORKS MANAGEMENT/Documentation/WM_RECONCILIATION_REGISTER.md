## 0  What this register is, and what it is not

The brief sets the order of authority for Works Management:

1. **the owner's uploaded Works Management files** ← the four files in `USER_SOURCE/`
2. actual project information
3. existing project documentation
4. previously generated Works Management material ← **WM1**

So **nothing of the owner's has been corrected, re-derived, rounded or rebuilt.**
The BOQ, the rates, the cost build-up, the durations, the dates and the activity
structure are published exactly as supplied, straight out of the owner's own files
(`Cost/` and `Programme/USER_MASTER_CONSTRUCTION_SCHEDULE_R0.*`).

**Every arithmetic statement in this register is checked, not asserted.**
`Scripts/wm2_user_package.py` re-adds the owner's own columns and writes the result to
`QAQC/WM2_SOURCE_AUDIT.txt`. Run it and the figures below either reproduce or they do not.

> **RULED BY RC1, 10 September 2026 (master Part H.14).** When this register was first
> written it recorded the conflicts and resolved none of them. The user has since
> instructed that every inconsistency be removed by choosing the best option available,
> so **R-1 to R-14 have all been ruled on** — the rulings, with the basis for each, are
> in master **K.1c** and are summarised in **§9** at the end of this document.
>

## 6  One WM1 item that the project has now superseded

`SP-06` in the WM1 bill reads *"RC lintels over openings, **200 × 150 PROVISIONAL —
design required**"*, tagged `[N]`, with the note that **no lintel design existed
anywhere in the project**.

That is no longer true. **SP-B2** (10 September 2026, master **A.4.8** and **H.12**)
designs lintel **L1** to IS 456 — **190 × 150, M30 / Fe500, 2-T10 bottom, 2-T8 top,
T6 links @ 150, bearing 200 each end**, one type over all eleven openings — and closes
**WM-V5**. The tie detail closes **WM-V11**.

L1 is **190 wide, not the provisional 200**, so the WM1 lintel volume is over-measured
by 5 %. WM1's own run of lintel — eleven openings, each clear width plus 200 bearing
each end — is **17.000 m**, so:

| | |
|---|---:|
| `SP-06` provisional, 200 × 150 | 17.000 × 0.200 × 0.150 = **0.510 m³** |
| **L1 as designed, 190 × 150** | 17.000 × 0.190 × 0.150 = **0.485 m³** |
| Over-measure | **0.026 m³ of M30** |

**`SP-06` is left exactly as it stands in the WM1 bill and is not re-measured here** —
WM1 is a preserved revision, and 26 litres of M30 changes nothing. It is recorded so
that the next revision of the bill picks up L1, with its reinforcement, rather than a
provisional section with none.

**What WAS corrected in WM1, and why.** The package said in **eleven places** that *no
lintel design and no wall tie detail exist anywhere in the project*. SP-B2 designed
both, so those statements became false — and a Works Management package that tells a
builder a design does not exist, when it does, is worse than one with a stale quantity.
The statements were corrected at their generators and the package rebuilt. **The tie
spacing was a genuine contradiction**: WM1 assumed *"every fourth course"* as an
explicit placeholder, SP-B2 designs **every fifth course (≈ 450)**; the placeholder is
superseded and recorded as such. **No quantity, rate, duration, date, float or resource
changed** — `Underground_Shelter_BOQ.csv`, `Underground_Shelter_WBS.csv` and the
programme CSV are byte-identical, and the package's own audit still runs **65 checks,
65 pass**. Full file list in master **Part H.12**.

Each has a reason, and none of them makes the owner's bill wrong:

- **R.C.C. wall casting.** The owner prices every grade of concrete at one blended
  ₹8 640/m³, which does not distinguish a wall from a raft. The SSR does: a raft is
  ₹7 562 and a wall is ₹16 025, because **wall formwork** is what the wall item is dear
  for. The owner's rate is close to the SSR's **raft** rate and is being applied to walls.
- **Rock excavation.** ₹850 blends murum and basalt into one figure and carries **no
  depth increase**. The SSR's non-blasting rock item is ₹1 307 before the Section B
  increases of +20 % and +30 % that a 6.8 m excavation attracts.
- **Reinforcement.** ₹78 000/t against the SSR's ₹89 703/t for TMT Fe-500 fixed in
  position.

**A rate below schedule is a commercial position, not an error.** What did not exist
before was the comparison; it exists now, so the position can be taken knowingly.

## 10.3 One structural correction to the cost build-up

The owner's estimate adds **10 % Contractor's Overhead and Profit** on top of its own
rates. The SSR General Notes state that a completed rate **already contains** 10 % for
overheads, 10 % for contractor's profit and 1 % labour cess. So that head must not be
added again to an SSR-priced bill, and WM4's recapitulation does not add it. The three
heads are listed explicitly as NOT ADDED, with the clause, in
`Cost/WM4_COST_SUMMARY.csv`.
