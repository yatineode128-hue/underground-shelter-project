WHAT A 'COMPLETED RATE' ALREADY CONTAINS, on the SSR's own authority
  General Notes, Section A:
    'For labour amenities and all other overhead charges, 10 % provision
     is considered in Rate Abstract.  In addition, 10 % provision for
     Contractor's Profit is also considered separately.'
    'Labour Cess at 1 % has been considered separately in Rate Abstracts.'
    'Material Rates are exclusive of GST.  Rates for completed items are
     also exclusive of GST.  While preparing the estimates, separate
     provision for GST shall be made in recapitulation sheet.'

  So the on-cost already inside every completed rate is
    (1 + 0.10) x (1 + 0.10) x (1 + 0.01) = 1.2221
  and overhead, profit and labour cess MUST NOT be added again in the
  recapitulation.  The owner's RC1 estimate adds 10 % contractor's
  overhead and profit on top of its own rates; against SSR rates that
  would be a double count.  WM4 does not make it.

  Every SSR concrete item reads 'including STEEL CENTERING, FORMWORK,
  cover blocks ... (EXCLUDING reinforcement and structural steel)'.
  So formwork is inside the concrete rate and reinforcement is not.

1.  HIGHER CONCRETE GRADE THAN THE SSR PUBLISHES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The SSR publishes R.C.C. pardi (wall) and staircase waist slab only to
M-25.  The shelter box is M-35.  The SSR's own instruction, General
Notes Section B:

    'If higher Concrete grade is required for any specific work, the
     rate analysis of the same shall be derived by adding difference in
     standard cement consumption in relevant SSR item's rate and shall
     be got approved from the Superintending Engineer, PWD of concerned
     circle.'

Standard cement consumption, SSR 'Consumption of Material' (bags/m3):
    M15   6.00 bags
    M20   7.00 bags
    M25   7.50 bags
    M30   8.00 bags
    M35   8.25 bags
    M40   8.50 bags

Cement at the SSR general rate Rs 6000 / M.T. = Rs 6.00 / kg; bag = 50 kg.

    uplift = d_bags x 50 kg x Rs 6.00/kg x 1.2221

THE FORMULA IS VERIFIED AGAINST THE SSR'S OWN PUBLISHED STEPS.
Where the SSR does publish consecutive grades, the difference it prints
must equal what this formula computes:

    step        formula   SSR prints (four item families)
    M20 -> M25   183.32    183 (25.11->25.13), 184 (25.13 fnd), 183, 183
    M25 -> M30   183.32    184 (25.15), 183 (25.35), 183, 183
    M30 -> M35    91.66     91 (25.17), 92 (25.37), 92, 92

The formula reproduces the schedule to within the rupee it rounds to.
That is the check that makes the derived rates usable.

2.  DEPTH AND FLOOR-LIFT INCREASES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SSR General Notes Section B, for BUILDING works:
    no increase
    3.0 m to 4.50 m depth add 20 %
    4.5 m to 6.0 m depth add 30 %
    Depth beyond 6.0 m — extra percent to be decided by the concerned Superintending Engineer

    Floor lift: ground 0 %, first 1 %, second 2 %, third 3 %, fourth 4 %

THE SHELTER EXCAVATION GOES TO (-)6.800, WHICH IS PAST THE SSR's LAST
DEFINED BAND.  The schedule hands that decision to the Superintending
Engineer and this package does not take it: the 6.0-6.8 m band and the
sump pit below it are priced at the 4.5-6.0 m band's +30 % as a DECLARED
LOWER BOUND, tagged [A], and raised as open item WM4-V1.

ROCK IS BROKEN, NOT BLASTED.  WM_CONSTRUCTION_METHODOLOGY.md: 'Rock is
removed by HYDRAULIC BREAKER, not by blasting ... controlled blasting
would require a vibration regime the project has not specified', and
risk S-02 says the same.  So SSR 21.20 (chiselling, wedging, line
drilling) governs and the three blasting items 21.17 / 21.18 / 21.19 —
Rs 869, Rs 1 033 and Rs 1 322 — DO NOT APPLY TO THIS PROJECT.
