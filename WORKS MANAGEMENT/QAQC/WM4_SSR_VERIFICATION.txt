SSR RATE VERIFICATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every rate in wm4_ssr_library.py was extracted from the SSR PDF and then
re-verified a second time against the raw text of the page it sits on.
30 of the 33 first-pass items matched automatically.  Three were checked
by hand against the page image text:
    21.33  Rs 7 / 7          confirmed (single-digit rates)
    21.40  Rs 1 454 / 436    CORRECTED from a mis-read 7 695 / 2 165
    39.50  Rs 5 700 / 1 809  CORRECTED from a mis-read 5 716 / 1 448
NEITHER CORRECTION TOUCHES A PRICED LINE: 21.40 is carried for
reference only, and 39.50 belongs to SP-12, which is NOT PRICED for
want of a window height.  Both are in the library so that the next
revision starts from a verified figure.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS AUDIT DOES NOT CLAIM
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * It does not check the QUANTITIES.  Those are WM1's, unchanged, and
    WM1's own 65-check audit covers them.
  * It does not claim the SSR item chosen for a line is the only
    defensible one.  Where a choice was made it is stated on the line.
  * A district cost index has NOT been applied.  The SSR is a STATE
    schedule; its General Notes give every Superintending Engineer the
    power to vary rates for local conditions, and no Pune circle
    variation is in this project.  Open item WM4-V4.
