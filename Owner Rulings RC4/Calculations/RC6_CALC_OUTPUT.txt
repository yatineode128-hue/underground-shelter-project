==============================================================================
  R.10  WM-V9 -- BENCH OR BATTER THE SOIL CAP
==============================================================================

RULING: keep the vertical face in rock; batter or bench ONLY the soil
overburden above rockhead.

WHY THIS IS THE RIGHT PLACE TO CUT.  The project measures 1.000 m working
space and VERTICAL UNBENCHED faces for the full 6.800 m.  In basalt that is
reasonable.  But SG1 measured what is actually on top of the basalt:

    rockhead                      0.9 - 1.5 m below grade        [C] off-site
    black cotton CH horizon       0.18 - 1.0 m, FSI 60-65 %      [C] off-site
    murrum below it to rockhead                                  [C] off-site

So the face is about 1.0-1.5 m of SOIL -- including very-high-swelling clay --
standing vertically on 5.3-5.9 m of rock, and the rock excavation UNDERCUTS
it.  Under the SG2-V5 acceptance the excavation also stands open across a
monsoon.  A vertical face in FSI 60-65 % clay, undercut and wetted, is the
one part of this excavation that has no business being vertical.

  EXCAVATION AS MEASURED
    box external            22.000 x 6.200 m
    + 1.000 m working space each side -> 24.000 x 8.200 m at formation
    depth, grade 0.000 to formation (-)6.800 = 6.800 m
    soil cap to batter, worst-case rockhead  = 1.500 m

  ADDITIONAL EXCAVATION, battering the top 1.500 m
        batter   offset         top plan   extra volume
         1 : 1   1.500 m   27.00 x 11.20        76.4 m3
      1.25 : 1   1.875 m   27.75 x 11.95        96.7 m3

    64.40 x 1.000 wide x 1.500 deep          = 96.6 m3

  ADOPTED -- BATTER THE SOIL CAP, VERTICAL IN ROCK BELOW IT

    Batter    1 : 1 MINIMUM over the soil cap, from grade down to rockhead,
              all four sides.  Vertical face retained in rock below rockhead.
    Extra     about 76 m3 at 1:1, rising to about 118 m3 at 1.5:1   [R]
    Working   UNCHANGED.  The 1.000 m working space is at FORMATION, 5.3 m
              below the battered zone, so no BOQ working-space quantity moves.

  *** AND THE PART THAT IS NOT MINE TO SETTLE. ***
  1 : 1 is stated as a MINIMUM, not as the answer.  A batter in FSI 60-65 %
  clay, undercut by rock excavation and exposed across a monsoon, may need
  to be considerably flatter, or need the cap stripped wider and replaced.
  IS 3764 is named BY TITLE ONLY -- it is not in this workspace and master
  Part G forbids citing a clause that cannot be confirmed.  THE BATTER ANGLE
  AND ITS SEASONAL VARIATION ARE THE GEOTECHNICAL ENGINEER'S, and the
  quantity above moves with the angle.  [A]

  *** AND THE SAME COLLISION SG-V6 HAS. ***
  This produces MORE excavated CH clay -- about 76-118 m3 of it, on top of
  the 8 m3 from the stairwell raft.  SG-V7, which the owner LEFT OPEN, warns
  against re-laying that material as the cover's 300 turf over the granular
  filter.  RECORDED, NOT RESOLVED -- and the stockpile is now an order of
  magnitude bigger than when SG-V6 raised it.

  WM-V9 CLOSES as a measurement basis -- the project now states a face
  treatment instead of an unexamined vertical.  The slope-stability
  ASSESSMENT it always asked for is still outstanding, and still needed.
