#chapter DESIGN OF STAIRCASES, HEADHOUSE AND CLOSURES | (MAIN STAIRCASE, ENTRY STAIRWELL, HEADHOUSE, BLAST DOORS AND ESCAPE SHAFTS)

#head Main Staircase

P1 Basis | The main staircase is within the stair shaft. The blast pressure in the shaft acts on the shaft walls and the blast doors; it does not produce a net load on the stair slabs, which are open on both faces. The staircase is therefore designed to IS 456 with the normal partial safety factors for the static loads.

P1 Flight | Each flight has a waist of 200 mm and spans between the landings.

P1 Opening Corner | At the top of the flight the tension face of the slab turns through an obtuse angle of 209°. A bar bent round such a corner has the resultant of its bend force directed out of the concrete and would spall the cover. The main bars are therefore not bent round this corner; each layer is continued straight, crossed and anchored L<sub>d</sub> = 640 mm into the opposite face, and a U-bar T16 @ 200 is added across the corner (SP 34, Cl. 5.5). At the foot of the flight the corner closes (151°) and the bars are bent normally.

#head Entry Headhouse

P1 Roof Slab (500 mm) | The headhouse roof is a 4 000 x 5 000 mm panel monolithic with 400 mm walls on all four sides (l<sub>y</sub>/l<sub>x</sub> = 1.25) and is designed for 396.5 kPa. It has been analysed by three methods and the most conservative has been adopted.

#calc
d = 500 - 75 - 10 ;; 415 mm
M<sub>u,lim</sub> = 0.133 x 43.75 x 1000 x 415² ;; 1 002 kNm/m
Method 1, one-way fixed-fixed strip, M<sub>p</sub> = w L<sub>n</sub>² / 16 = 396.5 x 4.0² / 16 ;; 396.5 kNm/m (adopted)
Method 2, IS 456 Table 26, Case 1, α<sub>x</sub> = 0.045 ;; 285.5 kNm/m
Method 3, yield line, fixed on four edges ;; 162.2 kNm/m
A<sub>st</sub> required (Method 1) 1 879 mm²/m; minimum 600 mm²/m
!Provided T20 @ 150 EF EW = 2 094 mm²/m, M<sub>u</sub> = 438.5 kNm/m ;; Utilisation 90 %
x<sub>u</sub>/d = 0.174 < 0.46 ;; Ductile
!Shear
V at d = 396.5 (2.000 - 0.415) ;; 628.4 kN/m
τ<sub>v</sub> = 1.514 N/mm²; τ<sub>c</sub> = 0.502 N/mm² (static M35, p<sub>t</sub> 0.505 %) ;; Links required
V<sub>us</sub> = 420.0 kN/m; A<sub>sv</sub>/s<sub>v</sub> = 2.327 mm²/mm; spacing limit 300 mm
!Provided T12 4-legged links @ 175 in the end 1 200 mm each side, @ 250 elsewhere ;; OK
#end

T The yield line capacity for a slab fixed on four edges is w<sub>u</sub> = 48 m / [a² (√(3 + (a/b)²) - a/b)²], which reduces to the fixed-fixed strip value w L²/16 as b tends to infinity. The one-way strip value has been adopted because it does not depend on full fixity of all four edges under a blast load. The roof has no opening. Its top steel is continuous over every support and anchored L<sub>d</sub> = 800 mm into the walls, and 300 x 300 mm haunches with diagonal T16 @ 150 are provided at all four wall-roof junctions. Corner torsion steel (IS 456 Cl. D-1.8) is not required, since all four edges carry full hogging steel continuous into the walls.

#head Blast Doors

P1 Requirements | Blast Doors 1 and 2 are proprietary tested assemblies, each 1 200 x 2 100 mm, rated not less than 7 bar, gas-tight and rebound-rated. Each door and its frame performs four functions at the same time:-

P2 Positive Pressure | The door takes the full 383 kPa from the stair shaft, in the same plane as the 400 mm wall in which it is set.

P2 Rebound | The negative phase of the blast and the rebound of the structure pull the leaf away from its frame. The door and its latching resist load in both directions.

P2 Gas-Tightness | The door is part of the envelope tested at +300 Pa. The seal seats on the steel frame, not on the concrete.

P2 EMP Continuity | The frame is cast in and welded to the reinforcement cage, so the cage is continuous around the opening. The same rule is applied to the frame of the headhouse security door and of the gas-tight door D-05 in W5.

P1 Door D-05 in Wall W5 | Wall W5 separates the plant room (bay 5), which contains the activated carbon filter beds, from the airlock (bay 6), and the primary escape route passes through it. It is fitted with a gas-tight, fire-rated door D-05, which is not blast rated since there is no pressure difference across W5.

#head Escape Shaft Heads and Ladders

P1 Hatch Loading | Each escape shaft is a 1 400 mm bore through the pressure slab, within a 250 mm collar of 1 900 mm outside diameter, with its head at +0.150 (ESC 1) or +0.700 (ESC 2). The hatch at the head closes an opening in the protective boundary and is designed for the full blast pressure of 383 kPa.

#calc
Force on the leaf = 383 x π x 0.700² ;; 589.6 kN
Moment, circular plate simply supported, M = w a² (3 + ν) / 16, ν = 0.30 ;; 38.71 kNm/m
Shear at the seating = w a / 2 ;; 134.1 kN/m
Flat Fe250 plate, t = √(6M / σ) = 30.5, say 32 mm; mass of a 1 600 dia x 32 mm leaf ;; 505 kg
#end

P1 Hatch Adopted | A flat plate of 505 kg cannot be opened by a person climbing out of the shaft. The hatch is therefore a ribbed steel weldment, with the following particulars:-

P2 Leaf | 1 600 mm diameter, 12 mm face plate, 8 radial ribs 150 x 10 mm and a 150 x 12 mm perimeter ring; indicative mass about 322 kg.

P2 Seating | A steel seating ring cast into the 250 mm collar, with 150 mm bearing all round.

P2 Fastening | Four quarter-turn dogs, so that the leaf resists uplift due to the negative phase and rebound as well as downward pressure.

P2 Operation | Counterbalanced or spring-assisted, and openable from inside by one person without a key or tool.

P2 EMP Bonding | The leaf, the seating ring and the collar reinforcement are electrically continuous, and the hatch is procured as a single item covering both its structural and its bonding requirements.

P1 Ladders | One ladder type serves both shafts, with the following particulars:-

P2 Rungs | 20 mm diameter galvanised mild steel rungs, 400 mm clear width, at equal pitch within each shaft: 297.6 mm over 21 spaces in ESC 1 and 295.7 mm over 23 spaces in ESC 2.

P2 Stringers and Fixing | Two 50 x 10 mm galvanised flat stringers, fixed by cast-in lugs to the collar and the roof slab bore, with expansion-anchored brackets at 1.5 m centres over the lower 3.2 m.

P2 Clearances | Not less than 200 mm behind the rungs and 750 mm clear climbing space in front, with grab rails extending 1 100 mm above the head.
