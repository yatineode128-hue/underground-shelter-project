#chapter FIRE AND LIFE SAFETY | (FIRE STRATEGY, COMPARTMENTATION AND EVACUATION PLAN)

#head Fire Strategy

P1 Basis | The shelter is a sealed underground volume of 332.8 m³ supplied with 300 m³/h of filtered air, about 0.9 air changes per hour, and it has no smoke vent, since every opening in the envelope is a blast valve or a blast door. The fire strategy is therefore to prevent fires, to detect them early, to extinguish them while they are small, and to evacuate by a route away from the fire if a fire cannot be controlled.

P1 Features of the Design | The following features of the design support the strategy:-

P2 Generator Compartment | The largest fire load, the generator with its fuel and exhaust, is in bay 8 behind Blast Door 2 in the 400 mm wall W7, separated from the occupied bays by the stair shaft.

P2 Separate Ventilation | Bay 8 has its own air path of 2 600 m³/h through BV-4 and BV-5, which does not connect with the gas-tight envelope, so that a generator fire is ventilated away from the occupied bays.

P2 Escape from Dead Ends | The two end bays, bays 1 and 8, each contain an escape shaft, so that no occupant is in a dead end without a way out.

P2 Short Travel | The longest travel distance to the primary route is about 14.6 m.

P1 Compartmentation | Bays 1 to 6 form one smoke compartment 20.8 m long, since the four W8 partitions are non-structural and have permanent 900 mm openings. Blast Doors 1 and 2, and wall W5 with door D-05, are the fire barriers within the shelter.

P1 Detection and Lighting | A fire detection and alarm panel and maintained emergency lighting are provided on the essential board and are supplied from the battery if both the mains and the generator fail.

P1 Water | The 1 000 litre tank in bay 1 is the 96-hour potable water supply for the occupants and is not used as firefighting water except on the orders of the commander.

P1 Immediate Actions | On discovering a fire the following actions are taken:-

P2 | The alarm is raised.

P2 | The fire is attacked while it is small, provided the route behind the person is clear.

P2 | The blast door between the occupants and the fire is shut.

P2 | The generator is stopped if the fire is in bay 8 or its origin is not known.

P2 | The occupants evacuate by the route given in Table 14.3, without passing the fire to reach a preferred route.

P2 | A roll call of the nine occupants is taken at the exit.

P1 Fire Party | With nine occupants, every occupant forms part of the fire party and is practised in the fire drill and the use of the escape routes and shaft ladders.
