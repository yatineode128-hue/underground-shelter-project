P1 Reasons for the Layout | All effluent is released downhill of, and away from, the buried box, whose backfill is more permeable than the surrounding rock. The grouping gives one percolation test location, one keep-clear zone and one common services trench, and the cover slabs and septic vent are 11 to 22 m from the shelter, so that they do not indicate its position.

P1 Clearances | The clearances required by IS 2470 are met: foul soak pit to septic tank 5.40 m against 5 m, and soak pit to the nearest building 10.97 m against 2 m. There is no well within 15 m of the foul soak pit.

P1 Pipe Runs | The external pipe runs are: septic tank to soak pit, DN100 at 1 in 100, 5.40 m; rising main from the service entry plate to the storm soakaway, DN50, 27.00 m; entry channel to the storm soakaway, DN100 at 1 in 100, 32.35 m, routed west clear of the stairwell excavation; and stairwell sump to its soakaway, DN50 rising main, 29.60 m.

P1 Air Shafts | The fresh-air intake shaft SH-1 (600 x 600 mm) is centred at (-2 400, 3 100), west of the box, and the generator air shaft SH-2 (600 x 600 mm) is at X 22 598 - 23 198, east of the box. #head Schedule of Finishes

P1 Finishing Rules | The finishes have been selected for decontamination, drainage and the protection of the envelope. The following rules apply throughout:-

P2 No Voids | No suspended ceiling, dry lining, boxing-in or cavity of any kind is provided anywhere in the gas-tight envelope. Every finish is applied directly to the concrete, so that every duct, pipe and surface can be inspected and decontaminated.

P2 Cast-in Fixings | No fixing is drilled into the tanked envelope. Bunk frames, handrails, cable trays, duct supports and door frames are fixed to cast-in sockets or frames only.

P2 Coved Junctions | Every junction in a wet or clean area, floor to wall, wall to soffit and around every gully and sleeve, is coved.

P2 Stair Finishes | The stair treads carry a cast-in non-slip nosing with no build-up on the tread, so that the 280 mm going is maintained.

#head Concealment

P1 Measures | The buried structure is concealed by the following measures:-

P2 Turf | The whole buried roof is covered by 300 mm of topsoil and turf (30.87 m³), re-laid from the 92.82 m³ of topsoil stripped from the site itself, so that the restored surface matches its surroundings.

P2 Ground Form | The finished grade over the box is crowned and falls at 1 in 50, a natural-looking fall, and the berms against the headhouse and stairwell are graded at 1.5 : 1 to read as ground.

P2 No Surface Drainage Features | There is no roof outlet, downpipe, manhole or gully anywhere on the buried roof; the cover drains through the granular filter on the sloping burster slab to the berm toe.

P2 No Spoil Heap | All surplus excavation (1 113.94 m³) is re-used on site as engineered fill and crushed rubble, and no spoil heap is left.

P1 Above-Ground Elements | The elements that stand above grade are the sentry post (parapet +7.000), the covered stairwell head (+2.450), the two air shaft goosenecks (+1.500), the headhouse roof (+0.900) and the escape shaft heads (+0.700 and +0.150). The sentry post is a manned observation post and is meant to be seen, and the headhouse is the entrance; the protected volume itself lies below ground under graded, turfed cover.
