#front ABSTRACT

T The project covers the planning, analysis, design and works management of a CBRN hardened underground operations room at the College of Military Engineering campus, Pune. The facility protects nine occupants for 96 hours against the blast of a nuclear air burst with a peak incident overpressure of 344.7 kPa (50 psi), together with chemical, biological, radiological and nuclear contamination, initial nuclear radiation, fallout and electromagnetic pulse.

T The protected volume is a buried reinforced concrete box 22.0 m long and 6.2 m wide, divided into eight bays and founded on Deccan basalt at a depth of 6.8 m, with 2.0 m of engineered cover including a reinforced concrete burster slab. Entry is through a covered stairwell, an entry headhouse and a main staircase to two blast doors rated at 7 bar, and two vertical escape shafts provide emergency exits. A two-storey framed sentry post on isolated footings completes the installation.

T The blast load has been derived using the dynamic load factor approach of IS 4991 with a ductility ratio of 5, giving a design pressure of 383 kPa on the roof and walls. With a roof natural period of 13.4 ms against a positive phase duration of 0.13 to 1.33 s, the response is quasi-static. All elements have been designed to IS 456 : 2000 by the limit state method, using the plastic mechanism moment and dynamic material strengths for the blast case, and the sentry post frame has been detailed to IS 13920 : 2016 for seismic Zone III. The governing element, the 900 mm pressure slab, carries T25 bars at 150 mm each face each way.

T The protective services comprise two 300 m³/h NBC filter trains with an overpressure cascade through a three-stage decontamination airlock, a shielded EMP Zone 2 enclosure designed for 80 dB from 10 kHz to 1 GHz, a tanked drainage system with a clean sump, a 15 kVA standby generator with battery-backed essential supply, and a fire and life safety plan with three escape routes. The works have been estimated at **Rs 2,97,90,913** and programmed for completion in 224 working days.
