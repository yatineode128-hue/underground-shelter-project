#chapter DESIGN OF THE UNDERGROUND STRUCTURE | (PERIMETER WALLS, PROTECTIVE WALLS, MAT FOUNDATION AND PRESSURE SLAB)

#head General

P1 Notation | In the reinforcement descriptions, T16 @ 150 EF EW denotes 16 mm Fe500D deformed bars at 150 mm centres, each face, each way, and 4L denotes four-legged links. Design values for the blast case use f<sub>ck,dyn</sub> = 43.75 N/mm² and f<sub>y,dyn</sub> = 625 N/mm² in flexure, and the static τ<sub>c</sub> of M35 in shear.

#head Perimeter Walls W1 to W4 (600 mm)

P1 Design | The perimeter walls span vertically between the mat and the pressure slab over a clear height of 3.2 m and are designed for the blast pressure of 383 kPa, which exceeds the static earth and water pressure at the base (83.2 kPa) by 4.6 times.

#calc

M<sub>p</sub> = w L<sub>n</sub>² / 16 = 383 x 3.200² / 16 ;; 245.1 kNm/m
M<sub>u,lim</sub> = 0.133 x 43.75 x 1000 x 517² ;; 1 556 kNm/m
A<sub>st</sub> required (f<sub>y,dyn</sub> 625, f<sub>ck,dyn</sub> 43.75) ;; 894 mm²/m
Minimum steel: vertical 0.0012 x 600 = 720; horizontal 0.0020 x 600 = 1 200 mm²/m (IS 456 Cl. 32.5)

!Provided T16 @ 150 EF EW = 1 340 mm²/m per face; M<sub>u</sub> = 362.8 kNm/m ;; Utilisation 68 %
x<sub>u</sub> = 46.3 mm, x<sub>u</sub>/d = 0.089 < 0.46 ;; Ductile
!Shear

τ<sub>v</sub> = 414.8 x 10³ / (1000 x 517) ;; 0.802 N/mm²

#end

P1 Thickness | The wall is 68 per cent utilised in flexure. The 600 mm thickness is also required for the 75 mm cover against rock, for two curtains of steel with closed links, waterstops and cast-in frames within one section, for crack control under a permanent head of water, for the EMP double curtain at 150 mm, and for the axial load of 1 168 kN/m from the roof.

#head Protective Walls W6 and W7 (400 mm)

P1 Loading | The stair shaft is open to the atmosphere through the 2 800 x 3 160 mm roof void, the headhouse and the entry stairwell. The time taken for the shaft to fill with blast pressure is compared below with the duration of the blast pulse.

#calc
Fill time = V / (A c) = 105 / (3.3 x 340) ;; 0.094 s
Positive phase duration, t<sub>d</sub> ;; 0.13 to 1.33 s
#end

T The shaft fills to the full overpressure in less than the shortest positive phase duration. Walls W6 and W7 therefore separate a pressurised shaft from bays 6 and 8 at ambient pressure, and are designed for 383 kPa acting from the shaft (load case BL3).

P1 Thickness | A 200 mm wall is not adequate for this load, since its limiting moment is below the demand whatever steel is provided:-

#calc
200 mm wall: d = 200 - 40 - 6 = 154 mm; M<sub>u,lim</sub> = 0.133 x 43.75 x 1000 x 154² ;; 138.0 kNm/m
Demand M<sub>p</sub> = 383 x 3.200² / 16 ;; 245.1 kNm/m
Two-way action, panel 3 200 x 3 800 fixed on three edges (yield line) ;; about 172 kNm/m
#end

T The walls have therefore been made **400 mm** thick.

P1 Design | The design of the 400 mm wall is as under.

#head Mat Foundation (600 mm)

P1 Bearing | The bearing pressure is 58.4 kPa in service and 404.9 kPa under blast, i.e. 1.8 and 12.5 per cent of the safe bearing capacity of 3 240 kPa, and 20.6 per cent of the lowest soaked rock value. Bearing does not govern.

P1 Flexure | Two cases have been considered:-

P2 Case 1, Net Uplift | Under combination 102 the net upward load over the 5.0 m span is 46.11 - 15.00 = 31.1 kPa, giving M<sub>p</sub> = 31.1 x 5.0² / 16 = 48.6 kNm/m, which is nominal.

P2 Case 2, Soft Band | A 3.0 m wide band of red-bole or vesicular rock is assumed to be lost at the worst position under the blast bearing pressure. This case governs.

#calc
q = 404.9 kPa over a 3.0 m unsupported width; M = q L² / 12 = 404.9 x 9 / 12 ;; 303.7 kNm/m
d = 600 - 75 - 8 ;; 517 mm
A<sub>st</sub> required ;; 1 115 mm²/m
!Provided T16 @ 150 EF EW = 1 340 mm²/m; M<sub>u</sub> = 362.8 kNm/m ;; Utilisation 84 %
#end

P1 Thickness | The thickness has been chosen from the study in Table 7.1. A 500 mm mat would require more steel than the 150 mm spacing provides, and 600 mm is adopted.

P1 Other Checks | The remaining checks on the mat are as under:-

P2 Punching | No column or pedestal bears on the mat; the headhouse walls bear on the roof. Punching shear (IS 456 Cl. 31.6) does not arise.

P2 Sliding and Overturning | The box is socketed about 4.8 m into basalt and the annulus is backfilled with M15 lean concrete, and the lateral loads on opposite faces are symmetrical. Neither sliding nor overturning is a possible mode of failure.

P2 Settlement | The foundation is compensated (net pressure change at formation -105 kPa) and rests on sound basalt. Settlement is negligible.

P1 Flotation | The box is lightest during construction, before the roof and the cover are in place. The factor of safety against flotation at each stage, at the design groundwater table, is given in Table 7.2 and Fig 7.4. The factors are independent of the box length, since weight and uplift both vary in proportion to it.

T The following measures are specified for construction:-

P2 Dewatering | Continuous dewatering from the start of excavation until the backfill and the cover are complete.

P2 Relief Plugs | Six temporary pressure-relief (knock-out) plugs in the mat, grouted only after backfilling.

P2 Programme | The substructure is programmed to be completed and backfilled in the dry season, before the monsoon.

P2 Backfilling | Backfill placed symmetrically on all sides in layers.

#head Pressure Slab (900 mm)

P1 One-Way Action | The roof panel is 20.8 m long and 5.0 m wide, an aspect ratio of 4.2. IS 456 Table 26 is tabulated only up to 2.0, so two-way action is not claimed and the slab is designed to span one way across the 5.0 m width. The moment therefore depends only on the width; lengthening the box adds no roof moment, whereas widening it from 5.0 to 6.0 m would increase the moment by 44 per cent.

P1 Flexure | The slab is designed for the total load of 448.15 kPa (combination 103).

#calc
d = 900 - 75 - 12.5 ;; 812.5 mm
M<sub>p</sub> = w L<sub>n</sub>² / 16 = 448.15 x 5.0² / 16 ;; 700.2 kNm/m
M<sub>u,lim</sub> = 0.133 x 43.75 x 1000 x 812.5² ;; 3 841 kNm/m
A<sub>st</sub> required ;; 1 632 mm²/m
Minimum steel 0.12 % x 900 = 1 080 mm²/m; maximum bar diameter D/8 = 112 mm ;; OK
!Provided T25 @ 150 EF EW = 3 272 mm²/m; M<sub>u</sub> = 1 362.4 kNm/m ;; Utilisation 51 %
x<sub>u</sub> = 113.0 mm, x<sub>u</sub>/d = 0.139 < 0.46 ;; Ductile
#end

P1 Shear | The shear at the support and the direct shear are checked as under.

#calc
V at the support face = 448.15 x 2.50 ;; 1 120 kN/m
V at d from the face = 448.15 (2.50 - 0.8125) ;; 756.3 kN/m
τ<sub>v</sub> = 0.931 N/mm²; τ<sub>c</sub> = 0.450 N/mm² (p<sub>t</sub> 0.403 %); τ<sub>c,max</sub> = 3.70 N/mm² ;; Links required
V<sub>us</sub> = 390.8 kN/m; A<sub>sv</sub>/s<sub>v</sub> = 1.106 mm²/mm; spacing limit 300 mm governs
!Provided T12 4-legged @ 250 in the end 1 500 mm each side; T12 2-legged @ 300 elsewhere ;; OK
!Direct shear (UFC 3-340-02 Sec. 4-30)
V<sub>d,max</sub> = 0.16 f<sub>ck,dyn</sub> b d ;; 5 688 kN/m
Applied 1 120 kN/m ;; 19.7 %, OK
#end

T Direct shear is a blast-specific mode in which a very short, high shear pulse shears a slab at its support before the flexural response develops. It has been checked to UFC 3-340-02, since IS 456 does not cover it.

#head Sump Pit and Internal Walls

P1 Clean Sump SU-01 | The sump pit in bay 5 is 1 500 x 1 500 x 1 500 mm internally (3.375 m³), with its invert at (-)7.600 and base slab at (-)8.000. The walls are 300 mm and the base 400 mm thick, reinforced with T16 @ 150 each face each way, and the pit is cast monolithic with the mat in the same continuous pour. The opening in the mat is trimmed with 4-T20 each face each side, anchored L<sub>d</sub> 800 beyond, and the tanking membrane is dressed around the pit.

P1 Wall W5 and Partitions W8 | Wall W5 (200 mm) is a fire and gas-tight wall with no pressure difference across it, and carries T12 @ 150 each face each way. The four W8 partitions are 110 mm non-structural walls reinforced with A252 mesh on both faces.
