#chapter DESIGN OF THE SENTRY POST | (SLAB, BEAMS, COLUMNS, FOOTINGS, LINTELS AND WALL TIES)

#head General

P1 Design Basis | The sentry post is an observation post outside the protective boundary. It has been designed as a conventional reinforced concrete moment-resisting frame for gravity, seismic and wind loads, to IS 456 : 2000, IS 1893 (Part 1) : 2016 and IS 13920 : 2016, in M30 concrete with Fe500 reinforcement. All members are designed for the base shear of 73.18 kN.

P1 Blast | The post is not designed for blast. The reflected pressure of 1 366 kPa on its 4.0 x 6.7 m face amounts to about 36 600 kN, and resisting it would need about 700 mm of reinforced concrete on all four above-ground faces, which is not justified for a peacetime observation post. The protection of the shelter does not depend on the post in any way.

#head Slab S1 (150 mm, Two-Way)

P1 Design | The slab is a two-way panel on beams at both floors, designed for the first-floor load of 11.625 kPa, which governs.

#head Isolated Footing F1 (1 500 x 1 500 x 600)

P1 Founding | The footings are founded on in-situ basalt at (-)2.000, below the black cotton soil horizon and clear of the backfill of the shelter excavation.

P1 Base Pressure | The base pressures are worked out as under.

#calc
!Service
P = 167.5 (column) + 36.1 (seismic axial) + 33.75 (footing) ;; 237.4 kN
M = 29.3 kNm; e = 0.123 m < L/6 = 0.250 m ;; No tension
q<sub>max</sub> = 105.5 (1 + 6 x 0.123 / 1.5) ;; 157.6 kPa
Against 3 240 kPa (4.9 %) and the soaked rock value 1 961 kPa (8.0 %) ;; OK
!Ultimate, 1.5 (DL + EL)
P<sub>u</sub> = 1.5 (148.4 + 36.127) = 276.8 kN; M<sub>u</sub> = 1.5 x 29.27 = 43.9 kNm
e<sub>u</sub> = 43.9 / 276.8 = 0.159 m < 0.250 m; average pressure 276.8 / 2.25 = 123.0 kPa
q<sub>u,max</sub> = 123.0 (1 + 6 x 0.159 / 1.5) ;; 201.1 kPa
#end

P1 Design of the Base | The base is designed conservatively for the maximum pressure acting over the whole cantilever.

#calc
Cantilever from the column face = (1 500 - 350) / 2 ;; 575 mm
M<sub>u</sub> = 201.1 x 0.575² / 2 ;; 33.2 kNm/m
d = 600 - 50 - 8 ;; 542 mm
A<sub>st</sub> required 142 mm²/m; minimum 0.12 % x 600 = 720 mm²/m ;; Minimum governs
!Provided T12 @ 150 both ways bottom (754 mm²/m) ;; OK
One-way shear at d from the face (33 mm from the edge): V<sub>u</sub> = 6.6 kN/m, τ<sub>v</sub> = 0.012 N/mm² ;; OK
Punching at d/2 (IS 456 Cl. 31.6.1): b<sub>0</sub> = 4 x (350 + 542) = 3 568 mm
V<sub>u</sub> = 276.8 - 123.0 x 0.892² = 178.9 kN; τ<sub>v</sub> = 0.093 N/mm² < 0.25 √30 = 1.369 N/mm² ;; OK
Anchorage of column starters: L<sub>d</sub> (compression) = 37 x 16 = 592 mm; available 526 + 128 (bend) = 654 mm ;; OK
#end

T The 600 mm depth is governed by the anchorage of the column starter bars, not by bending or shear.

#head Lintels and Wall Ties

P1 Lintel L1 | One lintel type, L1, is used over all eleven openings in the sentry post (ground floor door D1 900 and window W1 1 200; first floor door D1 900 and eight vision-panel openings 1 200 wide). The lintel carries only the masonry above it, since the floors and roof are carried by beams B1 and B2.

#calc
Section 190 x 150, M30 / Fe500, cover 30, bearing 200 each end
Effective span = min (clear + d, bearing centres) = min (1 315, 1 400) ;; 1.315 m
Masonry height at the 60° arching limit = 0.866 x 1.315 ;; 1.139 m
w = 0.190 x 20 x 1.139 ;; 4.33 kN/m
M = 0.935 kNm, V = 2.85 kN; M<sub>u</sub> = 1.403 kNm against M<sub>u,lim</sub> = 10.03 kNm ;; 14 %
A<sub>st</sub> required 29.5 mm²; minimum 0.85 b d / f<sub>y</sub> = 37.1 mm² ;; Minimum governs
!Provided 2-T10 bottom (157 mm²), 2-T8 top, T6 2-legged links @ 150 ;; OK
τ<sub>v</sub> = 0.195 < τ<sub>c</sub> 0.56 N/mm² ;; Nominal links only
#end

T The lintel has been designed for masonry standing just below the 60° arching height, which is the heaviest load any opening height can produce, so the design covers every opening in the post.

P1 Wall Ties | The brick infill is tied to the columns with 6 mm mild steel ties at every fifth course (about 450 mm) up both column faces, projecting 200 mm into the bed joint and anchored to the column by a cast-in or drilled and grouted 10 mm dowel. The top course is built tight to the beam soffit and the joint packed, consistent with the response reduction factor of 3.0 adopted for a frame with unseparated infill.
