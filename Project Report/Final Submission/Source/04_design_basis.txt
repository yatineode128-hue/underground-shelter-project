#head Design Philosophy

P1 Limit State Design | All elements have been designed by the limit state method of IS 456 : 2000. The blast case is treated as an equivalent static load obtained from the dynamic load factor (Chapter 5), which is valid because the response of the structure to the long-duration nuclear blast pulse is quasi-static.

P1 Blast Case | For the blast combination the following provisions of IS 4991 have been applied, with all section capacities computed to IS 456:-

P2 Load Factor | The blast load is applied with a load factor of 1.0, since it is an extreme event checked against ultimate capacity with dynamic material strengths.

P2 Dynamic Strength | The characteristic strengths of concrete and steel are increased by 25 per cent for flexure (IS 4991 Cl. 10.3.1): f<sub>ck,dyn</sub> = 43.75 N/mm² for M35 and f<sub>y,dyn</sub> = 625 N/mm² for Fe500D.

P2 Shear | No dynamic increase is taken on shear (IS 4991 Cl. 10.3.1.1). Every τ<sub>c</sub> is taken at the static M35 value.

P2 Bond | The 25 per cent increase in bond permitted for the blast case is not taken; all development and lap lengths are the static values.

P2 Ductility | A ductility ratio μ = 5 (moderate, repairable damage) is adopted. The sections are kept under-reinforced (x<sub>u</sub>/d well below 0.46) and every blast-governed element carries designed shear links, so that the ductile flexural mode governs.

P1 Other Cases | The static, uplift, serviceability and construction cases are designed with the partial safety factors of IS 456 Table 18, and the sentry post to IS 456 and IS 13920 with the seismic combinations of IS 1893.

P1 Choice of Grade | M35 is the minimum grade for the very severe exposure condition (IS 456 Table 3 and 5), which applies because the structure stands permanently below the design groundwater table. Fe500D is used for the shelter because the D grade guarantees the minimum elongation on which the ductility ratio of 5 depends.

P1 Bar Spacing | The spacing of the main bars in both curtains of every element of the box is limited to 150 mm each way. This is closer than IS 456 Cl. 26.3.3 requires and has been adopted so that the reinforcement cage gives useful low-frequency EMP shielding (Chapter 11).

#head Concrete Mix Design

P1 Method | The concrete mixes have been proportioned by the method of IS 10262 : 2019 (Annex A) for M35 (shelter, stairs and headhouse) and M30 (sentry post and burster slab). The pressure slab is cast in a single continuous pour of 112 m³ into a congested cage (T25 at 150 mm in both curtains with four-legged links), and the M35 mix is therefore designed for pumping at 100 mm slump.

P1 Test Data for Materials | Specific gravity of cement 3.15, coarse aggregate (basalt) 2.84, fine aggregate 2.65 and admixture 1.145. Standard deviation s = 5.0 N/mm² (IS 10262 Table 2). Entrapped air 1.0 per cent for 20 mm aggregate (Table 3).

P1 Target Mean Strength | The target mean strength is the greater of f<sub>ck</sub> + 1.65 s and f<sub>ck</sub> + X (IS 10262 Cl. 5.2).

#calc
M35: 35 + 1.65 x 5.0 = 43.25; 35 + 6.5 = 41.50 ;; f′<sub>ck</sub> = 43.25 N/mm²
M30: 30 + 1.65 x 5.0 = 38.25; 30 + 6.5 = 36.50 ;; f′<sub>ck</sub> = 38.25 N/mm²
#end

P1 Water/Cement Ratio | The free w/c ratio has been taken as 0.40 for M35 and 0.44 for M30, within the limit of 0.45. The lower value for M35 is adopted because the structure is permanently below the water table and its external face can never be inspected; a dense, low-permeability matrix is also required for the crystalline waterproofing admixture to be effective.

P1 Water Content | IS 10262 Table 4 gives 186 kg/m³ for 20 mm aggregate at 50 mm slump, increased by 3 per cent for every 25 mm of additional slump.

#calc
M35: 186 x 1.06 (100 mm slump) = 197.16; less 20 % for superplasticiser = 157.7 ;; 158 kg/m³
M30: 186 x 1.03 (75 mm slump) = 191.58; less 18 % for superplasticiser = 157.1 ;; 157 kg/m³
#end

P1 Cement Content | The cement content follows from the water content and the w/c ratio.

#calc
M35: 158 / 0.40 = 395.0, adopted 400 kg/m³; free w/c = 158 / 400 = 0.395 ;; 400 kg/m³
M30: 157 / 0.44 = 356.8, adopted 360 kg/m³; free w/c = 157 / 360 = 0.436 ;; 360 kg/m³
Check: 400 and 360 lie between the minimum (340 / 320) and the maximum (450) ;; OK
#end

P1 Proportion of Coarse Aggregate | IS 10262 Table 5 gives 0.62 for 20 mm aggregate with zone II sand at w/c 0.50, corrected by 0.01 for every 0.05 change in w/c. For the pumped M35 the value is reduced by 10 per cent (Cl. 5.5.1).

#calc
M35: 0.62 + 0.01 x (0.50 - 0.395) / 0.05 = 0.641; x 0.90 = 0.577 ;; CA 0.577, FA 0.423
M30: 0.62 + 0.01 x (0.50 - 0.436) / 0.05 = 0.633 ;; CA 0.633, FA 0.367
#end

P1 Trial Mixes | The proportions are confirmed by trial mixes before the first structural pour. The trials establish workability after the transport time, 7 and 28 day strength against the target mean strength, the compatibility of the superplasticiser with the crystalline admixture, and the behaviour of the mix at the placing temperature. Water and aggregate batch weights are corrected on site for the free moisture and absorption of the aggregate.
