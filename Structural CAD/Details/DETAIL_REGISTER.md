## 3 — DETAILS THAT ARE **NOT** ISSUED, AND WHY

| Detail that a reader might expect | Why it is absent |
|---|---|
| **Column sections, column/footing details, beam–column joints** | **No RC column, framed beam or beam–column joint exists in the underground shelter.** The box is a monolithic plate structure; master B.3 declares punching NOT APPLICABLE for exactly this reason. Recorded on R-001 and R-401 |
| **Isolated footing, foundation beam, pedestal, pile cap details** | None exists — the foundation is a continuous mat |
| **Blast valve trimming detail ×5** | Sizes and sleeve details are on sheet **S-06** and are **not in the design register**. **NOT DETERMINABLE.** Generic rule on R-003 D2 |
| **Service-entry plate detail** (W2 at X ≈ 11 800) | Plate size **[NOT AVAILABLE]** in master Part B. **NOT DETERMINABLE** |
| **CBRN duct / ventilation penetration details** | No penetration schedule exists in the project record. **NOT DETERMINABLE** |
| **Blast-door leaf, frame section and anchor detail** | Vendor item, **[NOT AVAILABLE]**. Only the *structural* interface is drawn — cast-in frame, welded to the cage for EMP continuity. **No proprietary requirement is invented** |
| **Stairwell roof / headhouse roof junction** | **C16 is UNRESOLVED** — the master's own documents disagree on whether that roof is 250 or 500. **No junction detail is drawn until a ruling is given.** Recorded on R-803 |
| **Sentry-post anything** | **Out of scope by instruction** |

> **A detail is not drawn where the structural design basis is insufficient.** The generic
> trimming rule on R-003 D2 is issued so no penetration is left with *no* guidance, and it is
> labelled on the sheet as **not a substitute for a designed detail**.
