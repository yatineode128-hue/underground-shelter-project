> **RULED — RC2, 11 September 2026 (master H.21). THE GENERATOR MAY RUN DURING MODE 3.** As issued, mode 3 said *"all five blast valves shut"* while mode 5 required *"BV-4 and BV-5 open"* and was called *"independent of modes 1–4"*. BV-4 and BV-5 are two of the five, so the two statements could not both hold during mode 3, and the project held no position (EL-V1). **The project owner has ruled: all five valves shut at the shock and hold 1.3 s; BV-4 and BV-5 are then REOPENED for generator operation.** Bay 8 is outside the gas-tight envelope, so the clean zone is unaffected — which is what the mode 5 note always said. **Mode 3's 48 h limit is unchanged: it is set by the soda lime, not by power.** Two consequences are recorded, not resolved: the electrical battery is confirmed at Case A, 149 Ah (master H.20, EL1); and **reopening BV-4/BV-5 leaves two DN350 bores open in the post-attack period — bores that EM1 showed fail both EMP criteria — which sharpens EM-V3, whether bay 8 is inside the EMP boundary, without deciding it.**

**Mode 5 is independent of modes 1–4.** The generator air path (BV-4, BV-5, Bay 8) does not touch the gas-tight envelope — Bay 8 is outside it, behind Blast Door 2 and W7. Running the generator does not depressurise the clean zone and does not consume filter life.

**Mode 3 is limited by the soda lime, not the oxygen** (finding HV-F1): unscrubbed CO₂ alone gives 9.9 h, the 40 kg soda-lime store gives 48 h, the 15 m³ oxygen store gives 80 h. **The consumable to count on the drill card is soda lime.**

**Mode 1 is a gap on S-06** — no filter bypass for peacetime running is shown anywhere. Without one, every hour of peacetime ventilation spends carbon-bed life. **Not added here**: a bypass adds a leak path to the envelope and that is a protective decision (HV-D1).
