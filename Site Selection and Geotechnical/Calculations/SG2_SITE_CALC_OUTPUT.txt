S.1   SITE ORIENTATION  -  fixed by this revision
====================================================================================================

  PROJECT +X = EAST        PROJECT +Y = NORTH     [A]

  The project has worked in a local frame since Rev F (master A.4.1)
  and has never tied it to north.  Five reasons, all pointing the same
  way:

  1  ACCESS.  The covered entry stairwell's grade door is at its WEST
     end, X 9250 (A.4.7), so the approach comes from the west - and the
     CTW blocks, the roads and the campus are west of the plot (deck
     slides 13, 14, 17).  The entry faces the installation it serves.
  2  FALL.  SG1 read the ground as falling EAST / NE, 580 -> 575.  With
     +X east the drainage field is DOWNGRADIENT, so nothing recharges
     the ground upslope of a flotation-critical tanked box.
  3  INTAKE AND EXHAUST AT OPPOSITE ENDS.  SH-1 (fresh air) west,
     SH-2 (generator) at X 22598-23198 east - 22.6 m apart at minimum.
  4  NOISE AND SIGNATURE.  Bay 8 - generator, ESC 2, SH-2, BV-4/BV-5 -
     is at the east end, away from the campus.
  5  The sentry post covers the approach from the north.

  *** AND THE LIMIT ON REASON 3, STATED PLAINLY. ***
  NO WIND DIRECTION DATA EXISTS ANYWHERE IN THIS PROJECT.  The P1 deck
  gives monthly mean SPEED and no direction; there is no wind rose.  So
  the orientation cannot be justified on prevailing wind, and it is
  not.  What it does instead is put the intake and the exhaust at
  OPPOSITE ENDS OF A 22 m BOX, which is the robust choice WHATEVER the
  wind does.  A wind rose matters for more than this - it is also the
  plume direction for the CBRN case - and it is opened as SG2-V4.  [N]
